Good questions.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what your business needs: pages, languages, booking or checkout. After a short chat we send a fixed, written quote, usually within one business day. The concept is free either way.

Do you only build websites?

No. We also set up AI assistants and automations for WhatsApp and Instagram, and run ads and content. Many clients start with a website and add AI or marketing once it is live, but you can start with any of them.

What is the launch kit?

Everything that gets people to your new site: your Google profile set up, a QR poster for your counter, WhatsApp auto-replies, Instagram launch posts, a review link and your own domain and email. It comes with every Growth and Signature project.

Why is the concept free?

Because the work speaks better than a proposal. You look at a live website made for your business, and if it isn't right for you, you owe nothing.

We're in a different country. Does that matter?

No. Everything happens online: a live link to review, written updates and calls when you want them. We plan around your time zone. Our work so far spans Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore and Pakistan.

How long does it take?

It depends on the package and how quickly we receive your content. The timeline is written into the quote before we start, and we keep to it.

Who owns the website and domain?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, and once paid, the site and its code are yours.

Can I edit it myself?

Yes. Prices, opening hours, promos and photos can be changed from a simple panel on your phone.

Which languages do you build in?

We write in English and Bahasa Indonesia, and build in any language: you provide or approve the copy.
